Discover the heritage, precision engineering, and uncompromising standards behind every piece of National Fibre Glass tactical equipment. Since 1975, we have been forging the frontline of defense.

Legacy of Protection

Founded on the principles of absolute security and uncompromising durability, National Fibre Glass (NFG) has been at the forefront of tactical and industrial safety equipment manufacturing in India for decades.

Our specialization lies in the advanced engineering of Fiber Reinforced Plastic (FRP) and premium composite materials. This allows us to craft anti-riot shields and safety helmets that are not only extraordinarily resilient against high-impact forces, but also remarkably lightweight for the modern defender.

From our state-of-the-art manufacturing facilities to the frontlines of security operations, every NFG product is a testament to rigorous safety standards, sophisticated craftsmanship, and a relentless commitment to protecting those who protect us.
